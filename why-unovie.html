04 — Why now

Ready for 2027.

Several dated changes are turning industrial AI from a pilot into a requirement.

/pilots

Pilots need to become production

Most manufacturers are still in pilots or limited deployments. Buyers want a partner who reaches production in a quarter, on hardware built for the job.

/rules

New factories, new scrutiny

Factory ramps in 2027–28 arrive with battery-site safety rules, fab equipment-security standards and defense data-handling requirements. Buyers need monitoring and evidence, not just dashboards.

/governance

Governance decides what survives

AI governance rules — including Texas's, which recognises the NIST AI RMF — reward systems that can show their work. Every Unovie site ships a signed evidence pack mapped to NIST SP 800-53, the NIST AI RMF and IEC 62443.
