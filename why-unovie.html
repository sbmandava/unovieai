02 — Built for Physical AI

From bits to atoms.

Physical AI means AI that reads the real world through its signals and acts on real equipment — so it has to be fast, local and safe. That is what AgentMesh and AgentNexus are built for.

01

Perceive

Vibration, electrical, battery, process and network signals, read where they start.

02

03

Act

Low-consequence actions directly; safety-related actions as requests your systems decide.

04

Adapt

Tested improvements every night, signed and reversible — the base model never drifts.
