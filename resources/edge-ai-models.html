Summary

Most AI advice assumes a big cloud and unlimited compute. Edge projects get neither. This guide starts with the basics: what a model is and how training works. It then explains why edge projects stall, and how to plan around the limits of local hardware before you spend money.

Who it is for

Enterprise architects and engineering leads who make decisions about AI models but are not ML researchers.

What you will learn

  • How AI models learn, explained in plain words and without math.
  • Why "just fine-tune it" often fails on edge hardware.
  • How to tell if your model is too small or is just missing the right context.
  • How to test changes so you never fool yourself about progress.
  • How to think about cost, governance and risk before you scale.

The full 25-chapter guide also covers how Unovie builds and runs custom models on site. We share it with engineering teams as part of a technical call.
