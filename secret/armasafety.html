Core pitch · 30 seconds

"We're Unovie, an Austin company. We put AI on the factory floor that watches your robots, conveyors and drives and catches problems early, like a gearbox wearing out or a motor running hot, so you fix it on your schedule instead of in a breakdown.

The part safety people like: the AI never moves equipment. Anything touching safety goes to your control system and your operators. And every decision is recorded, so you can show exactly what happened and why."
