Robot & Motion Health: catch a failing robot joint before it stops your line.

AgentMesh reads joint current, torque, vibration and cycle data from the robot controllers, servo drives and motion axes on every cell, and compares each joint with its own history. When a joint or gearbox starts to drift, parts from that cell can be sent to rework within 30 ms (target), and a work order with the evidence attached goes to maintenance.

The repair gets planned instead of forced by a stopped line. Stopping or changing the robot's motion always stays with the cell controller, the safety PLC and the operator.
