New to Unovie? Here's what we build, and how a project with us starts.

We build Physical AI for the factory floor: custom Edge-AI Agentic Systems for battery, chip fab, space, robotics, defense and energy manufacturers.

AgentMesh works at the machine. It reads what your robots, cameras, sensors and test rigs produce and catches problems early. AgentNexus works across your sites. When one plant finds a problem, it checks the others and drafts the maintenance plans and work orders for your team to approve.

Your people stay in charge. A language model is never on the path that moves equipment, and every decision is recorded.

How we start:
1. A site audit at no charge. An engineer spends a day or two on your floor, then sends written findings and a pilot plan.
2. A six-week paid pilot on your own line and your own data, with a measured results report. It's credited toward production.
3. Production in about a quarter, on Unovie's custom-built AMD or NVIDIA hardware accelerators.

Want to see how it works? Our engineers can walk you through it.
