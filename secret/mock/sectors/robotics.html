01 · The problem

What gets in the way.

Drift scraps parts first

A worn gearbox or a drifting servo shows up as bad parts long before the robot faults.

Too much data to watch

Every cell streams joint current, torque, cycle and vision data. Nobody can watch all of it.

Fleets that go dark

Drones and autonomous vessels spend hours or days without a link. Health checks have to run on board and catch up later.
