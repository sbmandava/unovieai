01 — The problem

What gets in the way.

/downtime

Utility trips scrap wafers

A failed dry pump or scrubber can take a tool down mid-process. The warning signs are in the vibration and power data, days ahead.

/staff

Ramp with thin crews

New fabs and supplier plants open with fewer experienced facilities engineers than they need.

/ip

Design and process IP

Fab data is among the most guarded in industry. Cloud analytics is usually a non-starter.
