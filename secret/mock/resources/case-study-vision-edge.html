Case study · Vision at the edge · Semiconductor

A thousand cameras, and every decision made on site.

A semiconductor manufacturer wanted cameras watching every chamber on its lines: 25 cameras per chamber, 40 chambers per site. Nobody can watch a thousand feeds, and sending them all to the cloud would have been slow, expensive and a risk for sensitive footage. They had only a couple of dozen labelled images to start from. We proved the approach in an MVP on their own footage, analysing more than 300 frames per second on site, then designed the site so the vision AI runs next to the cameras, every feed is checked as it arrives, and the video stays in the building.

02 · The problem

Too many cameras, not enough eyes.

Nobody watches the feeds

Cameras get installed for safety and quality, then mostly record. Problems are found later, on a replay, after the parts are scrapped.

The cloud adds delay

A round trip to the cloud adds delay and depends on the network link. A decision that matters on the line can't wait for either.

Costs grow with every camera

Per-camera cloud pricing looks small at ten cameras. At a thousand, it becomes one of the biggest lines in the budget.

Models go stale

Lighting, fixtures and products change. A model set up once and left alone gets worse every month.

03 · What we built

Watch every feed, flag what matters.

Decides next to the camera

Each feed is checked as it arrives, on hardware in the customer's building. No cloud round trip, and it keeps working if the internet link drops.
