01 · The problem

What gets in the way.

Ramp-up scrap

A new line learns as it runs. A bad weld found at end-of-line test has already cost a finished module.

Storage-site safety reviews

Fire codes, insurers and safety reviews ask for evidence that warning signs were watched, every hour, on every rack.

Process data is the IP

Cell chemistry, weld schedules and formation recipes can't go to someone else's cloud.
