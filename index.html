03 — The Unovie platform

Two systems. One agent-native platform.

AgentMesh keeps the fast loop at the machine. AgentNexus works at business speed across every site. Same agents, same models, same safety boundary — on purpose-built AMD or NVIDIA hardware that Unovie provides, designed for GPU and agentic workloads.

AgentMesh · at the machine

AI on your equipment's signals, next to the control system.

A Sovereign Pod — four servers with 2 × 10GbE networking and NVMe storage — on the factory network reads robot, vision, electrical, battery and test signals, decides inside a 30-millisecond budget, and acts only where the consequence is low. Anything safety-related becomes an alarm and a request to your PLC, safety system and operators. Every decision lands in a signed Evidence Log.

AgentNexus · across every site

Agents that work across sites, and get better from production.

One rack in a central cabinet hosts thousands of agents in sealed sandboxes, connects to every AgentMesh pod and turns line and site events into analysis, plans, quality records and work orders within half a second. New skills are tested before they go live. It never moves equipment.
