FAQ · For buyers

Straight answers, first.

What plant, quality, security and finance teams ask us before a pilot. The answer comes first.

01Does any of our data leave the site?

Not by default. AgentMesh is air-gapped on your factory network. Raw signals, images and models stay on site. Only what you choose goes out, such as work orders to your maintenance system. AgentNexus can connect to Claude and your ERP for business skills such as quotes and quality reports. That traffic goes through one logged gate you control, a named person approves every draft, and on controlled programs it stays off.

02What happens when the internet or site link drops?

Nothing changes at the machine. Each AgentMesh pod decides locally and keeps running. Findings for AgentNexus wait and sync when the link returns.

03Is Unovie a safety system?

No. AgentMesh is never on the path that moves equipment, and we make no SIL claim. It acts on its own only on low-risk outputs, such as a reject gate or a rework diverter. Anything safety-related becomes an alarm and a request. Your PLC, safety system and operators decide.

04What hardware does it run on?

Unovie's custom-built AMD or NVIDIA hardware accelerators. The AgentMesh pod is a compact 3U unit on AMD Ryzen AI Max+ 395. NVIDIA Jetson AGX Thor handles robotics and vision at the tool. NVIDIA DGX Spark is the engineering lab.

05Do we have to buy the hardware?

No. We supply it set up and tested. You can buy it, or take it as a service with no upfront hardware cost.

06What does a pilot cost?

The site audit is free: 1–2 days on site, then written findings and a pilot plan. The 6-week paid pilot is $40–60K. It is credited in full against a production site signed within 90 days.

07What do we get at the end of the pilot?

One domain kit running on your own data, a baseline ROI view, a measured latency report and a signed evidence pack of what the system did and why.

08How is production priced?

Per monitored asset and domain kit. Never per seat or per camera. We quote production after the pilot, when we both know the asset count and the value.

09How fast can we reach production?

Our target is about one quarter from the end of the pilot. The pilot's setup carries straight over.

10Which systems does it connect to?

Sensors, robot controllers, vision stations, test rigs, PLCs, MES and quality systems, historians, and work-order systems such as SAP and Maximo. Protocols include OPC UA, EtherCAT and Modbus. PLC connections are read-only except for the outputs you agree to.

11Do you support Anthropic's Model Hardware Standard (MHS)?

Not today. Anthropic's MHS is a research preview with no public spec, and we are not part of it. AgentMesh describes every device with its limits, and the pod enforces them. A language model never operates your equipment. We keep our device descriptions standard-neutral so we can adopt a published standard later.

12Is it certified or compliant with NIST, CMMC or IEC 62443?

Unovie is designed against NIST SP 800-53 and the NIST AI RMF. It produces evidence your team can use for SP 800-171 and CMMC or IEC 62443. Your assessor decides compliance, not a product.

13Does the AI change itself over time?

Only after testing. Nothing new switches on until it has proven itself on cases it has never seen. Every change is signed, logged and can be rolled back.

14Do you train on our data for other customers?

No. Models improve on your site, from your data. Nothing is shared back to Unovie or to other customers.

15How does it work inside?

Our engineers can walk you through how it works. Talk to our engineers through the contact page and we will set up a call.

16Where are your engineers?

Unovie is based in Austin, Texas. Our engineers work on site across Texas and serve customers nationally.
